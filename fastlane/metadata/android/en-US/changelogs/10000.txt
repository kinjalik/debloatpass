First release.

- Opens walletunion://, passeswallet:// and walletpasses:// links: downloads the real .pkpass and hands it to your wallet app (FossWallet, Google Wallet, …)
- Paste or share a link to the app, or save the card as a file
- Tells you when no app can open .pkpass and whether Google Wallet's import is off on your device
- English and Russian, light and dark theme
